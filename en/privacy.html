Privacy Policy

This policy describes the current handling of data in the WOOTREX app and on its website. For privacy questions, contact wootrex@seznam.cz.

Workout and body data

Workout data, body weight and body measurements are stored locally on the device. Physique photos selected or captured by the user are used and stored locally.

Location and device permissions

  • GPS is used to record outdoor activities.
  • The camera and photo library are accessed only when the user invokes a feature that requires them.
  • The microphone may be used only by functionality that explicitly requires recording.

You can grant or revoke permissions in your device settings.

Map tiles

When displaying a recorded outdoor route map, the app loads map tiles over HTTPS from OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org). The provider receives your IP address, technical request information and identifiers of the requested tiles, which can reveal the displayed area. The route is drawn on the device; this feature does not upload the complete GPS recording to OpenStreetMap. The provider describes its handling of requests in the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy.

Local data retention and deletion

Local data are used for workout history, activity recording and tracking body progress and remain in app storage until removed. Body records and their photos can be deleted in the app. Local app storage can be removed through the operating system's app-data removal or uninstall options; the exact options depend on the platform. Originals in the photo library, exported or shared copies and any system backups must be managed separately. WOOTREX cannot remotely delete data stored only on your device.

User-initiated sharing

If you export or share a transformation photo, the selected content is passed to the app or recipient you choose, and further processing is subject to their policies.

Tracking and data sharing

WOOTREX does not use advertising tracking and does not sell personal data. No WOOTREX analytics backend currently collects workout, body or location data.

Feedback

The website form and supported app versions send a message, category, optional email and limited source, platform and language data over HTTPS; the app also sends its version, build and screen. The API uses Cloudflare and is intended to store messages in D1 for handling feedback. Workouts, photos, GPS and account IDs are not automatically attached. Cloudflare processes IP and technical request data in transit. Send only content you choose to disclose; contact us about retention or deletion of messages and emails. Feedback delivery confirmation is not account-deletion confirmation.

This website

The WOOTREX website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate website traffic and page usage. This analytics is not used for advertising and does not use tracking cookies. WOOTREX does not use Google Analytics or advertising trackers and does not sell visitor data.

Beta link clicks

When you tap a beta testing button (Android APK or iOS TestFlight), the site anonymously sends an event to click.wootrex.cz. Only the platform type, language, source button, device type and server time are stored. No IP address, cookies, user agent or identifier is stored, events are not linked to a specific visitor, and a click does not mean a confirmed install.

Future changes

Changes to data processing will be published with an updated date. This preparation does not activate cloud history synchronization or paid purchases.

Delete your WOOTREX account

Account and Supabase

Optional accounts use Supabase Auth, processing email, account ID, authentication and verification data, and security-related technical data. Never send your password to support. Signing in does not upload workouts, GPS, photos or the profile avatar; these remain local.

Account and associated data deletion

Without installing the app, you can request deletion of your WOOTREX account and associated online data at wootrex@seznam.cz. The link opens your email application; you must send the message yourself.

Provide the email used for the account and request deletion of the account and associated data. Do not send passwords, verification codes or photos.

For earlier feedback or correspondence, identify the contact address you used. Ownership must be verified before processing. Use the same contact to ask about the procedure and timing.

Automatic deletion is currently disabled. Sending a request does not itself delete an account, and this page does not report completion of manual processing.

History, plans, measurements, photos and the local profile are on your phone; the website cannot remotely erase or back them up. Signing out or deleting an account is not a backup. To keep these data, do not uninstall the app or clear its storage merely to request account deletion. Manage exports, originals and system backups separately.

Manage any subscription in Google Play or the App Store. Cancelling it is not a prerequisite for requesting account deletion.

Support reviews the account and identifiable associated online data; other users’ data must remain intact. Ask about any necessary security or legal retention and applicable periods during processing. Immediate erasure of operational logs, provider backups or previously shared copies cannot be promised.
